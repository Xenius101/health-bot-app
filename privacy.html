Health Bot Privacy Policy

Effective 11/24/2026.

Health Bot is a personal tool used only by its owner, Ryan Scanlan.

  • It accesses only the owner's own Google Health weight and body fat data, read-only, through the scope googlehealth.health_metrics_and_measurements.readonly.
  • The data is used only to show the owner his own progress.
  • The data is stored privately in the owner's own assistant workspace.
  • The data is never sold, shared, or used for advertising or AI model training.
  • Health Bot follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
